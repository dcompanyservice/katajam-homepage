Word puzzle

Kata Jam is a relaxing word puzzle. Tap the front letter of a row and it flies into the word that needs it. Plan ahead so the waiting slots never fill up.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

How to play

  1. Tap the front letter of a row.
  2. It flies into an open word that needs it. If no word needs it, it waits in a slot.
  3. Fill every word to clear the level. If all waiting slots fill up, the level is lost.
  4. Gold, ice, bombs, chains, stars and keys appear as you go.

Features

  • Thousands of levels, checked so every level can be solved
  • Plays offline. No account, no sign-in
  • Words in English, Indonesian, Portuguese and Thai
  • 30 color themes to unlock with coins
