Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

dcompanyservice ("we") made the mobile game Kata Jam. This policy explains what information is handled when you play Kata Jam and visit this website.

1. What we collect

We do not collect personal information. Kata Jam has no account, no sign-in and no game server. Your progress (level, coins, themes, settings and language) is saved only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising

Kata Jam shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob (for example, to continue a level or double your coins). An ad plays only when you tap the button for it, and the game can be played without watching ads. Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, an approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, device information and diagnostic information under the Google Privacy Policy (how Google uses information from apps that use its services). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's consent tool before personalized ads are shown. On iOS, the game does not ask for tracking permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. Information collected by Google AdMob is processed by Google as described above. App downloads are handled by Google Play and the App Store under their own policies.

4. Children

Kata Jam is a general-audience word game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. Cloudflare and GitHub may record technical logs such as IP addresses for security (Cloudflare Privacy Policy, GitHub Privacy Statement).

6. Your rights

Because we hold no personal information, there is nothing for us to access or delete. To remove the data stored on your device, uninstall the game. For questions, contact us at the email below.

7. Changes

We will update the effective date at the top of this page when this policy changes.
